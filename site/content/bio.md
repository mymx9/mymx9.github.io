## About

I am a master's student in Applied Statistics at [Chongqing Medical University](https://www.cqmu.edu.cn/) (2024–2027, expected), working with Assoc. Prof. [Ximing Xu](https://pediatrixlab.com/) (PediatriXlab, Big Data Center for Children's Medical Care, Children's Hospital of Chongqing Medical University) on statistics for real-world health data.

My research sits at the intersection of statistical methodology and health data science: causal inference with observational data (cohort studies of vaccine effectiveness and safety), time-series forecasting for infectious disease surveillance, and statistical evaluation of generative models for tabular data. More recently I have also been working on medical imaging and multimodal learning: a clinically aligned three-stage cascade for pediatric pneumonia assessment (M.S. thesis, ongoing) and controllable chest X-ray synthesis based on latent diffusion models for rare-condition samples. I was trained in financial mathematics and spent three years in insurance data analytics before enrolling in my master's — a background that shaped my taste for turning messy real-world data into decisions.

I am currently **seeking doctoral positions in statistics** (Fall 2027). I am always happy to discuss research questions, data problems, or collaboration opportunities.
