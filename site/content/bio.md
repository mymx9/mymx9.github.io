- **Research interests**　Pediatric health data mining, medical applications of artificial intelligence, and statistical modelling of infectious diseases.
- **Selected work**　Two articles in *BMC Public Health* examine infectious disease forecasting and pertussis vaccination. The PedDialog manuscript on pediatric consultation dialogues is under review at *Scientific Data*.
- **Master’s thesis**　Under the supervision of [Ximing Xu](https://pediatrixlab.com/), this project aims to combine chest radiographs, blood counts and clinical histories to assess pediatric pneumonia using labels based on clinical diagnoses or guideline criteria.
